Before You Send

Use the right request type.

Blackshield Institute has books, curriculum tracks, membership access, interactive tools, and PDF/download assets. A strong request should name the product, page, issue, or goal clearly.

Product Access Mention the product title, page, tier, or PDF that you need help accessing.
Curriculum Request Mention the track, age group, learning goal, or curriculum product you are asking about.
Platform Issue Mention the page name, broken link, image issue, download problem, or browser/device being used.
